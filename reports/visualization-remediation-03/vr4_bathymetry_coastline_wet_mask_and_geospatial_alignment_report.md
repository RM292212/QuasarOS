# VR4: Bathymetry, Coastline, Wet Mask, and Geospatial Alignment Report

## Executive Summary
VR4 validates the geological context layer integrating authoritative GEBCO seafloor topography and high-resolution coastline polygons into the 3D scene.

## Key Geospatial Features
1. **Seafloor Mesh Alignment**: GEBCO bathymetry aligned with Copernicus horizontal resolution.
2. **Sub-Seafloor Occlusion**: Ocean volume rendering automatically terminates rays at the seafloor collision depth.
3. **Vertical Exaggeration**: Controlled scaling (1.0x to 50.0x) clearly marked in the UI.
4. **Orientation & Coordinate Grid**: 3D orientation gizmo and depth ticks provide precise spatial orientation.
